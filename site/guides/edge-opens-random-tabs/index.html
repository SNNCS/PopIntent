Microsoft Edge troubleshooting

Why does Edge open a new tab every time I click?

The cause depends on one detail: whether tabs appear only after you click on a particular website, or appear even when you are doing nothing. Work through this checklist in order.

Short answerTurn on Edge's built-in pop-up blocking, remove suspicious notification permissions and extensions, keep SmartScreen enabled, and scan the device if tabs appear by themselves. If a particular website attaches an unrelated popup to an ordinary click, a narrow click-intent tool such as PopIntent can cover that remaining pattern.

1. Identify which kind of “random tab” you have

  • A new tab opens immediately after a click: often a popup script or transparent click layer on that page.
  • The current tab changes destination: a same-tab redirect, link shortener, or server redirect.
  • Tabs appear when you are not interacting with a page: investigate notifications, unwanted extensions, or software on the device.
  • The visible link itself points somewhere unwanted: the website is doing what the link declares; a popup mismatch detector cannot decide whether that visible destination is honest.

2. Confirm Edge's built-in pop-up setting

  1. Open Edge Settings.
  2. Choose Cookies and site permissions.
  3. Open Pop-ups and redirects.
  4. Keep Block turned on and remove unfamiliar sites from the allowed list.

This is the correct first step. Microsoft notes that Edge's built-in blocker covers pop-up windows, but it does not block every advertising pattern or page redirect. See Microsoft's official Edge pop-up guidance.

3. Remove unwanted notification permission

Website notifications can look like browser-generated ads and may open a page when selected. In Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Notifications, remove or block sites you do not recognize. Do not click “Allow” on a site merely because it says permission is required to watch a video or prove you are human.

4. Review extensions and scan if tabs open by themselves

Open Extensions → Manage extensions. Disable unfamiliar items, then restart Edge and check again. If the behavior continues across unrelated sites or begins without a click, use Windows Security or another trusted security tool to scan the device. A page-level popup extension is not a substitute for removing unwanted software.

5. Keep Microsoft Defender SmartScreen enabled

SmartScreen checks suspicious pages and downloads against Microsoft's reputation systems. In Edge, find it under Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Security. It solves a different problem from popup blocking: reputation and phishing protection rather than comparing a click with the tab that appears. Read Microsoft's official explanation of how SmartScreen helps.

When PopIntent fits

If built-in blocking is enabled but a particular website still opens a different tab when you click Play, Download, Read more, or another visible control, PopIntent tests whether the opened destination agrees with the link you selected. It runs locally and closes a high-confidence mismatch.

It is not a general ad blocker, malware scanner, or guarantee against every same-tab or server redirect.
