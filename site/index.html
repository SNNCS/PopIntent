01 / What it does

Narrow protection,
clear decisions.

01

Understands your click

It compares the link you selected with the tab that actually opens.

02

Closes the mismatch

If the destination contradicts your click, PopIntent closes the unexpected tab.

03

Keeps recovery close

Use Open anyway for 60 seconds when you meant to visit the blocked destination.

04

Leaves ordinary browsing alone

Default mode permits ambiguous sign-in, payment, and document flows to reduce breakage.

02 / Privacy by construction

Your browsing stays
in your browser.

  • No account or backendThere is nowhere to send or sell browsing activity.
  • No analytics or telemetryThe extension makes no application-initiated network requests.
  • Domain-only local historyRecent blocks expire after seven days; private browsing is never persisted.
  • Open implementationPermissions, limits, source code, and threat model are public.

03 / Honest limits

Not another everything-blocker.

PopIntent is not a general ad blocker, malware scanner, or promise to stop every redirect. It focuses on a specific pattern: an unwanted popup or transparent click layer that contradicts what you selected.
